## 👋 Who we are

We're a student-run Hack Club. Every week we pick something to build, argue about how to build it, and ship it. You don't need any experience. Most of us started with none.

## 📅 When we meet

**Wednesday or Monday at lunch.** We email the exact day on Outlook each week.

## 🚀 Get your project on the wall

1. Make your project a public repo.
2. Add the topic `diab-hc-bakebuild` (gear icon next to About).
3. Hit **Submit a project** on the [gallery](https://innovate-diab.github.io).

Full steps are in the [gallery README](https://github.com/Innovate-DIAB/innovate-diab.github.io#readme).
